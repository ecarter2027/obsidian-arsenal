Armor redesigned as obsidian spines growing out of the body with glowing purple tips. Black Hole scales across tiers 1-10 (up to 20 blocks wide, 40 block pull, 50% execute chance, 30 s), only starts pulling once it hits something, and pulls far more blocks and mobs.

Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge 47+. Drop the jar into your `mods` folder along with GeckoLib, PlayerAnimator, AAA Particles and Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks.

_Jar-only release: the repository source reflects the latest version._
