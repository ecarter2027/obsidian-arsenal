Released with Obsidian EF Compat 1.3.0.

### Obsidian Gauntlet
- Reshaped claw with an **opposing thumb**: it comes off the palm and curls down toward the fingertips, so the claw closes like a gripping hand.
- Every finger now sits at its own angle. The index finger is nearly straight and fanned forward, and each finger behind it curls and fans back a little more.
- The **grappling hook** now has its own keybind, **H** by default, under "Obsidian Arsenal" in Controls. Press it again to let go.
  - It works with the gauntlet in either hand and in both modes.
  - Right-click no longer fires the hook.

### Epic Fight compatibility (Obsidian EF Compat add-on)
- **Right-click guards in battle mode**, like other Epic Fight weapons. Hold it to cross both claws in an X in front of your face.
- Blocked hits, parries and guard breaks all work with Epic Fight's guard and parry skills.

Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge 47+. Drop the jar into your `mods` folder along with GeckoLib, PlayerAnimator, AAA Particles and Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks (plus Curios and Caelus).
Optional: with Epic Fight, also add the attached `obsidian_ef_compat` jar.
