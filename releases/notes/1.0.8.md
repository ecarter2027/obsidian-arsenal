Obsidian Gauntlet grappling hook: right-click fires a glowing obsidian chain up to 80 blocks. Hook a block to be reeled in (no fall damage), hook a mob to yank it to you (bosses pull you to them instead); right-click again or sneak to let go. Players now glow while casting any Iron's spell, tinted per spell (school colour for spells from other mods).

Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge 47+. Drop the jar into your `mods` folder along with GeckoLib, PlayerAnimator, AAA Particles and Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks.
