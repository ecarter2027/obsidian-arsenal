First release. 10 spell scrolls (5 Obsidian, 5 Hellfire) as Iron's Spells schools, obsidian armor (better than netherite), and the two-mode Obsidian Gauntlet (multitool mining mode + 7-hit animated combat combo). Requires GeckoLib, PlayerAnimator, AAA Particles and Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks.

Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge 47+. Drop the jar into your `mods` folder along with GeckoLib, PlayerAnimator, AAA Particles and Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks.
