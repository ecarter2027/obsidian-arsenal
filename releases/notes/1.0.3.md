Black Hole becomes a charged projectile with an Interstellar-style lens (Iris/Oculus shader pack support) that pulls mobs within 20 blocks and tears up terrain. Plasma Beam insta-breaks the whole line of blocks you look along, with a rounded model and fast red/white streak texture. Whole-body gauntlet attack animations.

Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge 47+. Drop the jar into your `mods` folder along with GeckoLib, PlayerAnimator, AAA Particles and Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks.
