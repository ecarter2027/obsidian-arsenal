# Obsidian EF Compat

**Epic Fight moveset for Obsidian Arsenal's Obsidian Gauntlet.** This add-on turns the gauntlet into a full Epic Fight weapon with wild clawing combos, kicks, purple slash VFX, a claw guard, two signature skills and a new dodge.

Requires **Obsidian Arsenal** and **Epic Fight**. Forge 1.20.1.

---

## Claw-and-kick combos

Every attack is animated as a wide, wild claw swipe at full arm reach, with the whole body whipping into it. Kicks, spins, leaps and X-slashes are mixed in.

**One gauntlet (10 hits):**
1. Huge diagonal rake
2. Rising backhand into a front kick
3. Three-rake frenzy
4. Roundhouse
5. Spinning rake
6. Knee into a downward claw
7. Low sweep into a ground-cracking axe kick
8. Spinning hook kick
9. Leaping downward rake
10. Two-claw X-slash into a double smash

**Two gauntlets (10 hits):**
1. Right rake
2. Left rake
3. Twin rising rakes
4. Snap kick
5. X-slash
6. Spin rake
7. Left roundhouse into a rake
8. Four-claw frenzy
9. Axe kick into a double smash
10. Leaping smash into a spin

Kicks hit with the leg itself. Claws use blade hit effects and sounds, and kicks land with heavy blunt hits.

## Purple slash VFX
- Every claw and kick leaves a **purple trail**.
- Each strike throws a **purple slash crescent** along its swing, visible to everyone nearby.

## Guard
- **Hold right-click** in battle mode to guard, crossing both claws in an X in front of your face.
- Blocks, parries and guard breaks work with Epic Fight's guard and parry skills.

## Sprint and air attacks
- **Sprint attack:** a driving elbow strike with one gauntlet, or a running shred with two.
- **Air attacks:** a diving claw strike with one gauntlet, or a spinning two-claw dive that ends in a slam with two.

## Skills
- **Obsidian Impaler** (one gauntlet): the claw stretches into an obsidian spike that skewers everything in a line up to about 8.7 blocks ahead.
- **Twin Meteor** (two gauntlets): six alternating claw strikes, then a two-handed slam that cracks the ground and knocks enemies down.

## Dodge skill: Obsidian Stretch
- Your body **stretches** from where you stand to where you dash.
- The direction comes from your view and the movement keys (WASD). A key pressed just before the dodge still counts.
- **No cooldown** and no stamina cost. It works in the air and can chain.

## Battle-mode visuals
- A predator's guard stance with the claws held out, plus livelier walk and run animations.
- The gauntlet sits correctly on the hand in every Epic Fight pose.
- Obsidian Arsenal's armor keeps its **purple glow** and **obsidian spikes** in battle mode.
