# Changelog

## Obsidian Arsenal 1.0.11 + Obsidian EF Compat 1.3.0

### Obsidian Gauntlet
- Reshaped claw with an **opposing thumb**: it comes off the palm and curls down toward the fingertips, so the claw closes like a gripping hand.
- Every finger now sits at its own angle. The index finger is nearly straight and fanned forward, and each finger behind it curls and fans back a little more.
- The **grappling hook** now has its own keybind, **H** by default, under "Obsidian Arsenal" in Controls. Press it again to let go.
  - It works with the gauntlet in either hand and in both modes.
  - Right-click no longer fires the hook.

### Epic Fight compatibility (Obsidian EF Compat add-on)
- **Right-click guards in battle mode**, like other Epic Fight weapons. Hold it to cross both claws in an X in front of your face.
- Blocked hits, parries and guard breaks all work with Epic Fight's guard and parry skills.

## Obsidian Arsenal 1.0.10 + Obsidian EF Compat 1.2.9

### Obsidian Armor
- New model: slanted, sharp, plated "skin-armor" made of overlapping obsidian plates, replacing the old spiky look.
- New black, grey and purple color scheme, with purple seams that glow in the dark.
- The chest plates are now mirrored exactly, so the left and right sides match in shape and color.
- The armor spikes are now jagged 3D models with the vanilla obsidian texture, instead of stacked cubes.

### Obsidian Gauntlet
- New model: a smooth obsidian claw that grows out of the skin, using the vanilla obsidian texture.
- The claw is angled along the arm, with the palm toward the body and fingers along your side, and it now lines up with the arm correctly.
- The thumb is bigger and easier to see, and the whole gauntlet is about 20% larger.
- New item animations: the claws flex out while you're idle or moving, and snap open on every strike.

### Spells
- Spikes from **Obsidian Spike Explosion** and **Obsidian Storm** are now jagged 3D obsidian models with the vanilla obsidian texture.

### Epic Fight compatibility (Obsidian EF Compat add-on)
- **Visuals in battle mode:**
  - The gauntlet now sits correctly on the hand in every Epic Fight pose, including flying and other default poses.
  - The armor's purple glow and spikes now show in battle mode.
- **New combos:** every attack was reanimated as wide, wild clawing with full arm reach and the whole body whipping into each swipe. Kicks are mixed in: snap front kick, roundhouse, axe kick, rising knee and spinning hook kick.
  - **Single gauntlet (10 hits):**
    1. Huge diagonal rake
    2. Rising backhand into a front kick
    3. Three-rake frenzy
    4. Roundhouse
    5. Spinning rake
    6. Knee into a downward claw
    7. Low sweep into a ground-cracking axe kick
    8. Spinning hook kick
    9. Leaping downward rake
    10. Two-claw X-slash into a double smash
  - **Dual gauntlets (10 hits):**
    1. Right rake
    2. Left rake
    3. Twin rising rakes
    4. Snap kick
    5. X-slash
    6. Spin rake
    7. Left roundhouse into a rake
    8. Four-claw frenzy
    9. Axe kick into a double smash
    10. Leaping smash into a spin
  - Kicks hit with the leg itself, claws use blade hit effects and sounds, and kicks use heavy blunt hits.
- **Purple slash VFX:**
  - Every claw and kick leaves a purple trail.
  - Each strike throws a purple slash crescent along its swing that nearby players can see.
- **Sprint attacks:** an elbow strike with one gauntlet, or a running shred with two.
- **Idle and movement:** a predator's guard stance with the claws held out.
- **New dodge skill, Obsidian Stretch:**
  - Your body stretches from where you stand to where you dash.
  - The direction comes from your view and the movement keys (WASD). A key pressed just before the dodge still counts.
  - No cooldown or stamina cost, and it works in the air and can chain.
- More exaggerated, livelier walk, run, air-slash and skill animations.
